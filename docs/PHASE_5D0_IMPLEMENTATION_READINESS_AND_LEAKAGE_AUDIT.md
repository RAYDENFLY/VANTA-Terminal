## 2. Primary Signal and Trade Identity

### 2.1 Frozen Signal Generation Mechanics
In [`backend/strategy/candidates.py`](file:///d:/vanta-terminal/backend/strategy/candidates.py#L113-L230), `DonchianBreakoutStrategy.on_bar(bar, position)` executes causal streaming evaluation at each bar $t$ close:
- Lookback Channel $M$: Evaluates rolling High/Low across bars $t-M$ to $t-1$ (strictly excluding bar $t$):
  $$d_{\text{upper}} = \max_{k=1..M} H_{t-k}, \quad d_{\text{lower}} = \min_{k=1..M} L_{t-k}, \quad d_{\text{mid}} = \frac{d_{\text{upper}} + d_{\text{lower}}}{2.0}$$
- Volume Expansion Filter: Current volume must satisfy $V_t \ge \text{volume\_mult} \times \text{SMA}_{20}(V)_{t}$.
- Trend Filter: Close must be above/below EMA filter ($C_t > \text{EMA}_t$ and $\text{EMA}_t > \text{EMA}_{t-1}$ for Long).
- Volatility Hurdle: Wilder's $\text{ATR}_{14, t} > 0.0$.
- State Check: `not position.is_open()`.

When all conditions are met at bar $t$ Close, a `SignalEvent` is emitted:
- **Long Breakout**: $C_t > d_{\text{upper}} \land C_t > \text{EMA}_t \land \text{EMA\_UP} \implies$ `direction = PositionSide.LONG`, `stop_loss = round(C_t - 1.5 * ATR_t, 4)`, `take_profit = None`, `time_expiry_bars = 48`.
- **Short Breakdown**: $C_t < d_{\text{lower}} \land C_t < \text{EMA}_t \land \text{EMA\_DOWN} \implies$ `direction = PositionSide.SHORT`, `stop_loss = round(C_t + 1.5 * ATR_t, 4)`, `take_profit = None`, `time_expiry_bars = 48`.

## 3. Canonical Trade Outcome and Label Semantics

### 3.1 Binary Meta-Label Definition
Under the canonical execution rules of VANTA Terminal, each candidate trade $i$ has an immutable realized net profit/loss:
$$\text{NetPnL}_i = \text{GrossPnL}_i - \text{EntryFee}_i - \text{ExitFee}_i - \text{EntrySlippage}_i - \text{ExitSlippage}_i + \text{Funding}_i$$

The binary meta-label is defined strictly as:
$$y_i = \begin{cases} 
1, & \text{if } \text{NetPnL}_i > 0.0 \\ 
0, & \text{if } \text{NetPnL}_i \le 0.0 
\end{cases}$$

This definition guarantees that:
- The model trains directly on the true economic payoff of taking the trade under actual execution constraints.
- No artificial multiclass reversal or direction-inversion heuristics are required.
- Breakeven trades and trades whose gross gains are consumed by fees/slippage are classified as Class `0` (avoid taking).

### 3.2 Canonical Engine Execution Rules
Inspection of [`backend/backtest/portfolio_engine.py`](file:///d:/vanta-terminal/backend/backtest/portfolio_engine.py) and [`backend/backtest/execution.py`](file:///d:/vanta-terminal/backend/backtest/execution.py) reveals how candidate outcomes are simulated:

1. **Next-Open Entry Timing & Adverse Slippage**:
   - Order is submitted at bar $t$ Close ($T_{\text{close}, t}$) for execution at bar $t+1$ Open ($T_{\text{open}, t+1}$).
   - Execution price includes adverse taker slippage ($s = 0.0002$, 2.0 bps):
     $$P_{\text{entry}} = \begin{cases} O_{t+1} \times (1 + s), & \text{for LONG} \\ O_{t+1} \times (1 - s), & \text{for SHORT} \end{cases}$$
   - Entry commission fee is taker fee ($5.0\text{ bps} = 0.0005 \times \text{Notional}$).
2. **Direction-Specific Stop and Midline Geometry**:
   - Stop Loss price is anchored to $C_t \pm 1.5 \cdot \text{ATR}_{14, t}$ and transmitted via `SignalEvent.stop_loss`.
   - Take Profit is `None` (Strategy B relies on dynamic midline trend following).
   - Dynamic Midline Exit: At each subsequent bar $k > t+1$ Close, if $C_k < d_{\text{mid}, k}$ (for Long) or $C_k > d_{\text{mid}, k}$ (for Short), an exit order is queued and executed at bar $k+1$ Open with taker fee ($5.0\text{ bps}$) and adverse slippage ($2.0\text{ bps}$).
3. **Intrabar Barrier Evaluation & Conservative Stop-First**:
   - In [`ExecutionSimulator.evaluate_barriers`](file:///d:/vanta-terminal/backend/backtest/execution.py#L63-L130), for a Long position, Stop Loss triggers if $L_k \le P_{\text{SL}}$. For Short, Stop Loss triggers if $H_k \ge P_{\text{SL}}$.
   - If both Stop Loss and a profit target trigger in the same candle, `ExecutionPolicy.CONSERVATIVE_SL_FIRST` mandates that Stop Loss executes first.
4. **Gaps Through Stop Levels**:
   - If market opens beyond the stop level ($O_k < P_{\text{SL}}$ for Long, or $O_k > P_{\text{SL}}$ for Short), [`ExecutionSimulator`](file:///d:/vanta-terminal/backend/backtest/execution.py#L132-L165) fills at the gapped Open price plus slippage:
     $$P_{\text{fill}} = O_k \times (1 \mp s)$$
   - Fills never assume execution at the stop price during price gaps.
5. **Expiry & Terminal Fold-Boundary Flattening**:
   - Time Expiry: At bar $t+48$, if the position is still open, it is closed at bar Open ($O_{t+48}$) with taker fee and slippage.
   - Fold Boundary: At fold end (`oos_end_ms`), open positions are force-flattened at the final candle Close with taker fee (`exit_reason = BOUNDARY_FLAT`).
6. **Maker/Taker Fees & Slippage**:
   - Market entries: $5.0\text{ bps}$ fee + $2.0\text{ bps}$ slippage.
   - Market/Stop exits: $5.0\text{ bps}$ fee + $2.0\text{ bps}$ slippage.
   - Total baseline round-trip friction for market-in / market-out: $14.0\text{ bps}$.
7. **Funding**:
   - Frozen to `use_funding = False` in baseline protocol.
8. **Partial Fills & Rejections**:
   - Partial fills: Unsupported (full fills only).
   - Rejections: Logged with timestamps and reasons; rejected meta-signals consume $\$0.00$ collateral and incur $\$0.00$ fees.

### 3.3 Resolution of Price Anchoring Mismatch
The Phase 5C.4 report noted an inconsistency between `TripleBarrierLabeler` (which anchored barriers to $O_{t+1}$) and `MLModelStrategy` (which anchored barriers to $C_t$).

The timeline below defines when each price is known and how it is used:

```
Timeline: Bar t (Signal Bar) -> Bar t+1 (Entry Bar) -> Bars t+1..t+48 (Holding Period)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Bar t:
  - During Bar t: OHLCV streaming updates.
  - At Bar t Close (T_close,t):
    * C_t, H_t, L_t, O_t, V_t are fully known and sealed.
    * Indicators (Donchian channels, EMA, Wilder's ATR_14) are computed.
    * Feature vector x_t in R^21 is computed using data <= T_close,t.
    * Breakout conditions evaluated. If met, SignalEvent is emitted.
    * Reference price: C_t.
    * Stop Loss barrier set: P_SL = C_t - 1.5*ATR_t (Long) or C_t + 1.5*ATR_t (Short).
    * Meta-model predicts P_hat(y=1 | x_t).
    * If P_hat >= tau, signal is passed to engine queue; else dropped.

Bar t+1:
  - At Bar t+1 Open (T_open,t+1 = T_close,t + 1 ms):
    * O_{t+1} becomes known.
    * Market order executes at P_fill = O_{t+1} * (1 +/- s).
    * Position opens with entry_price = P_fill and stop_loss = P_SL.

Bars t+1 to t+48:
  - Intrabar monitoring against P_SL, Midline, and 48-bar expiry.
  - Position exits at T_exit with realized net PnL.
  - Realized outcome: y_i = I(NetPnL_i > 0).
```

**Conclusion**: Anchoring barriers to $C_t \pm 1.5\text{ ATR}_t$ at signal time reflects the exact, causal operation of `STRAT-B-01`. Entry fills occur at $O_{t+1} \times (1 \pm s)$. Because the meta-label is computed from the actual simulated trade ledger, there is zero mathematical mismatch.

### 5.2 Purging & Embargo Rules for Meta-Labeling
1. **In-Sample Candidate Selection Rule**:
   A candidate trade $i$ is eligible for In-Sample training if and only if:
   $$t_{i, \text{signal}} \ge \text{is\_start\_ms} \quad \text{AND} \quad t_{i, \text{exit}} \le \text{is\_end\_ms}$$
   Any candidate trade whose holding period extends past `is_end_ms` is **purged** from the training set. This guarantees that:
   - Zero training labels utilize price information beyond `is_end_ms`.
   - The 48-hour purge window ($172,800,000\text{ ms}$) contains no active training trades.
   - The 48-hour embargo window ($172,800,000\text{ ms}$) provides total separation before OOS.
2. **Out-of-Sample Candidate Selection Rule**:
   A candidate trade $i$ is evaluated in Out-of-Sample if and only if:
   $$t_{i, \text{signal}} \ge \text{oos\_start\_ms} \quad \text{AND} \quad t_{i, \text{signal}} < \text{oos\_end\_ms}$$
3. **Partition Isolation**:
   - Feature scaling via [`PartitionAwareScaler`](file:///d:/vanta-terminal/backend/ml/scaling.py) is fit strictly on In-Sample training features ($X_{\text{train}}$) and applied frozen to Out-of-Sample features ($X_{\text{eval}}$).

4. **Holdout Quarantine**:

## 7. Statistical Inference Plan

### 7.1 Paired Incremental PnL Formulation
For each candidate trade $i \in \{1, \dots, N_{\text{OOS}}\}$:
- Baseline realized return: $r_{0, i} = \frac{\text{NetPnL}_{0, i}}{\text{Notional}_{0, i}}$
- Meta-model realized return:
  $$r_{m, i} = \begin{cases} 
  \frac{\text{NetPnL}_{m, i}}{\text{Notional}_{m, i}}, & \text{if candidate } i \text{ is accepted} \\ 
  0.0, & \text{if candidate } i \text{ is rejected (filtered)} 
  \end{cases}$$
- Paired incremental return:
  $$\Delta r_i = r_{m, i} - r_{0, i}$$
  - If a losing trade is filtered ($r_{0, i} < 0$), $\Delta r_i = -r_{0, i} > 0$ (incremental gain).
  - If a winning trade is filtered ($r_{0, i} > 0$), $\Delta r_i = -r_{0, i} < 0$ (opportunity loss).

### 7.2 Stationary Block Bootstrap Specification
- **Resampling Unit**: Synchronized calendar-time hourly blocks ($t \in [T_k, T_k + L)$) across all 10 pairs simultaneously, preserving contemporaneous cross-sectional correlations.
- **Block Length Distribution**: Politis & Romano (1994) Stationary Block Bootstrap with geometric block length:
  $$P(L = k) = (1 - p)^{k-1} p, \quad p = \frac{1}{E[L]}$$
  with expected block length $E[L] = 120\text{ hours}$ (5 days) to capture multi-day volatility and trend persistence.
- **Bootstrap Replicates**: $B = 2,000$ replicates.
- **Deterministic Seed**: `seed = 42`.
- **Inference Metrics**:
  - $95\%$ bootstrap confidence interval for $\Delta \text{NetPnL} = \text{NetPnL}_{\text{meta}} - \text{NetPnL}_{\text{bench}}$.
  - One-sided $p$-value: $P(\Delta \text{NetPnL} \le 0)$.
  - Multiple testing correction: Holm-Bonferroni adjusted $p$-values across candidate models (`META-1`, `META-2`, `META-3`).
