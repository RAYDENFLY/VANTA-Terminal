## 1. Strict Event Timeline & Zero-Lookahead Protection

In quantitative research, lookahead bias (peeking into future price action or executing on bar data that has not yet closed) is a fatal flaw. VANTA Terminal implements strict causal event ordering:

### Timeline Axioms:
1. **Decision Time Barrier**: A trading signal generated by a strategy on bar $t$ consumes only closed price action up to $t_{\text{close}}$. It is generated at $t_{\text{close}}$ and **cannot be acted upon within bar $t$**.
2. **Execution Timing**: Any market order generated from a bar $t$ signal is queued for execution strictly at bar $t+1$ Open:
   $$t_{\text{execution}} = t_{\text{open}}(t+1) = t_{\text{close}}(t) + 1\text{ ms}$$
3. **No Intrabar Peeking**: High, Low, and Close of bar $t+1$ are unavailable when the order executes at Open.
4. **Verification via Permutation Tests**: Unit tests verify that modifying future bars ($t+5$) produces bit-for-bit identical executions and trade ledgers for earlier bars ($t \le 4$).

## 2. Same-Candle TP / SL Ambiguity Resolution

When a strategy sets both a Stop Loss ($SL$) and a Take Profit ($TP$) barrier, and both price levels fall within the High-Low range of a single historical candle:
$$\text{Low}_t \le SL \quad \text{and} \quad \text{High}_t \ge TP$$
Without tick-level order book data, the exact sequence of intrabar price touches is unknown.

### The Conservative Policy (`CONSERVATIVE_SL_FIRST`):
- **Core Principle**: In quantitative research, never assume the favorable outcome when ambiguity exists.
- **Resolution**: The simulator assumes the **adverse barrier (Stop Loss) is hit first**.
- **Audit Flag**: The trade is tagged with `is_ambiguous = True` in the trade ledger. This allows researchers to isolate and stress-test trades whose outcomes depend on intrabar pathing.
- **Configurability**: While configurable via `ExecutionPolicy`, `CONSERVATIVE_SL_FIRST` is the immutable system default.

## 3. Gap-Through-Barrier Execution Model

In real crypto-futures markets, weekend gap-opens, flash crashes, or news spikes can cause a new candle to open beyond a trader's stop price.

### Realistic Gap Fills:
1. **Long Position Gap-Down**:
   If a long position has a Stop Loss at $\$95.00$, and the next candle opens at $\$90.00$ ($\text{Open}_t < SL$):
   - Flawed simulators falsely fill at $\$95.00$ (an impossible price).
   - VANTA Terminal fills at:
     $$\text{Fill Price} = \text{Open}_t \times (1 - \text{slippage}) = \$90.00 \times (1 - 0.0002) = \$89.982$$
2. **Short Position Gap-Up**:
   If a short position has a Stop Loss at $\$105.00$, and the next candle opens at $\$110.00$ ($\text{Open}_t > SL$):
   - VANTA Terminal fills at:
     $$\text{Fill Price} = \text{Open}_t \times (1 + \text{slippage}) = \$110.00 \times (1 + 0.0002) = \$110.022$$
3. **Favorable Take Profit Gaps**:
   If a candle opens past a Take Profit limit barrier, the trade fills at the more favorable Open price without adverse slippage.

## 4. Time Expiry Semantics

When a strategy specifies a maximum holding period ($\text{time\_expiry\_bars} = N$):
- If the position is opened at bar $t_{\text{entry}}$, the expiry bar index is set to:
  $$\text{expiry\_bar\_index} = t_{\text{entry}} + N$$
- When the event loop reaches $\text{expiry\_bar\_index}$, if neither TP nor SL has been touched, the position is automatically closed at that bar's Open with market slippage, tagged with `exit_reason = TIME_EXPIRY`.
