### 6.1 Audit Verification Results

     $$\forall i \in \text{Candidates}: \max(\text{feature\_timestamps}_i) \le \text{signal\_timestamp}_i$$

     $$\text{ID}_{\text{IS}, k} \cap \text{ID}_{\text{OOS}, k} = \emptyset$$
   - **Result**: `partition_collision_count = 0` across all folds. Zero sample leakage between training and evaluation partitions.
3. **Purge Window & Boundary Crossing Behavior**:
   - In-Sample evaluation boundaries (`is_end_ms`) were audited to ensure no in-sample trade extended its outcome evaluation into the out-of-sample window.
   - All open positions reached natural resolution (stop loss, midline trailing exit, or 48-bar expiry) prior to `is_end_ms`.
